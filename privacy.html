Privacy Policy — Kenh Claude

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Who uses this app

Kenh Claude is a personal tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel it connects to. It is not offered to other users.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's permission, the app uses Google's read-only scopes (youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly) to read the owner's own YouTube channel information and analytics reports, such as views, watch time, subscribers gained, traffic sources and audience retention.

How the data is used

The data is used only to show the channel owner how their own videos perform. The app cannot upload, edit or delete any content.

Storage and sharing

Data and the sign-in token are stored only on the owner's personal computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the locally stored data from their computer.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the user support email shown on the app's Google sign-in screen.
